For an equestrian facility in the Liemers region, I developed a landscape planting design that responds to the area’s characteristic landscape structure and ongoing developments in the surrounding context. The principal challenge was to embed the site-specific layout, tailored to the needs of the horses, within the broader landscape framework. Shown here is part of the technical elaboration of the design.
