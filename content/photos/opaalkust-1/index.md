The [Côte d'Opale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C%C3%B4te_d%27Opale) provides hazy spring evening views of grain fields and undulating pastures against the backdrop of the English Channel and the North Sea. Describing the distinctive quality of its light, the painter Édouard Lévêque coined the name Côte d'Opale for this area in 1911.
