Designing private gardens is one of the most rewarding parts of my work. Whether you are reimagining an existing garden or starting from scratch, I create bespoke designs that respond to both the character of the place and the way you live. A selection of my work can be viewed <a href="portfolio/">here</a>.

As a guide, a bespoke design for a typical urban garden starts at € 750. A detailed planting plan, including species selection and an order list, starts at € 400. 

Following an initial meeting, I will provide a fee proposal tailored to your project. All fees include VAT and exclude travel expenses.
