In each project, I aim to create landscapes that unite ecological sensitivity and practicality. My work begins with a careful understanding of place, resulting in spaces that are both sustainable and attractive. I began my career as a lawyer at one of the Netherlands' leading law firms before joining the Dutch national electricity transmission system operator, TenneT. There, I saw the challenge of delivering essential infrastructure in a densely populated landscape with a rich history and uncertain future. That experience continues to inform my work today.

![me](/images/ik-1/ik-1.webp)

A longstanding interest in design and cultural landscapes led me to study Garden and Landscape Design at [HVHL University of Applied Sciences](https://www.hvhl.nl/opleidingen/hbo/tuin-en-landschapsinrichting/). Alongside my studies, I was responsible for coordinating the landscape design of a major high-voltage substation expansion and contributed to wider policy discussions. 

Today, I bring this combination of legal, technical, and design experience to my own practice, Van Campen Landscape: a landscape design studio specialised in utilities.
