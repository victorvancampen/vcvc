In every project, I aim to create landscapes that unite beauty, ecological sensitivity, and lasting practicality. My work begins with a careful understanding of place and the relationship between people and landscape, resulting in spaces that are both sustainable and attractive.

I began my career as a lawyer at one of the Netherlands' leading law firms before joining the Dutch national electricity transmission system operator. Working on the expansion of the electricity grid, I saw the challenge of delivering essential infrastructure while respecting the character of a densely populated landscape. That experience continues to inform my work today.

A longstanding interest in design and cultural landscapes led me to study Garden and Landscape Design at [HVHL University of Applied Sciences](https://www.hvhl.nl/opleidingen/hbo/tuin-en-landschapsinrichting/). Alongside my studies, I was responsible for coordinating the landscape design of a major high-voltage substation expansion and contributed to wider policy discussions. Today, I bring this combination of legal, technical, and design experience to my own practice, Van Campen Landscape: a landscape design studio specialised in utilities.
