Hi! This is a place where I keep a portfolio of my garden and landscape design work, and occasionally write about the subjects that inspire and fascinate me. A delight in design and a curiosity for plants first led me to study Garden and Landscape Design at [HVHL University of Applied Sciences](https://www.hvhl.nl/opleidingen/hbo/tuin-en-landschapsinrichting/). In every project, I seek to create landscapes that balance beauty, ecological sensitivity, and enduring practicality. 

My design approach is rooted in a careful understanding of place, seasonality, planting composition, and the relationship between people and landscape — with an emphasis on gardens that are both sustainable and atmospheric.
